# Discovery Checklist

Use read-only discovery to establish facts before planning or mapping. Do not run mutating cloud, Kubernetes, Terraform, Helm, or CI/CD commands without approval.

Capture, marking each item as fact or assumption:

- service or repository purpose, owner, and team
- provider, account/project/subscription, region, and environment
- networking, compute/runtime, managed services, storage/data, and IAM
- deployment path, IaC source, and CI/CD
- observability entry points
- known risks and open questions
