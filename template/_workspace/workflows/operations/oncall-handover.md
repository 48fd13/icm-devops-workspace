# On-call Handover Workflow

## Required context

- `_workspace/context/reference/incident-and-oncall-principles.md`

## Process

1. Collect open incidents and their state, alerts that fired or were silenced, risky or in-flight changes, and known degraded components.
2. List what the next on-call should watch, what is safe to ignore, and pending decisions with owners.
3. Link runs, dashboards, and tickets instead of copying evidence. Keep it sanitized.

## Result

Produce a handover note: current state, watch list, open decisions, and links.
